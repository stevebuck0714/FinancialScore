Phase 3: broaden AP payment-side ref filter to NOT-APV

Replaces the strict APP/APA payment allowlist with NOT-APV on the AP
control account. The AP balance change is conceptually "everything
posted to the AP control account except voucher creations" (which we
already get from APTransactionFact). The previous narrow filter missed
adjustments (Adj*, adj*), reversal pairs (Rev*/to*), and other manual
journal entries that legitimately move the AP balance.

Validated against prod TB checkpoints - drift trims by ~$4K at each
checkpoint (Jan/Feb/Mar 2026: +$162K/+$269K/+$111K vs Phase 2's
+$166K/+$273K/+$115K). Small but strictly directionally correct.

Phase 3 also explored a GL-only AP roll-forward as an alternative
architecture. Rejected: the pre-Apr-2023 GL data gap means payments
for vouchers created before our GL window hit the GL without their
offsetting voucher creations, producing ~$1M of phantom AP reduction
(drift -$946K to -$1.1M). Keeping the hybrid AP-fact + GL-payment
architecture is the correct call.

The residual ~$110K-$270K Phase 3 drift is timing-related (vouchers
and payments straddling month boundaries differently between our DB
and CSI's TB) and represents <1% of monthly AP throughput. Further
reduction would require a CSI-native period-balance feed which we do
not currently have.

Diagnostic scripts retained in tmp/ for future reference.
