Construction sector dashboard Phase 1: M3, M4, M5 mock-driven tabs

Adds full mock-driven implementations for the three remaining
construction-native tabs that were scaffolded in M1. All four tabs now
share a single seeded job set per companyId so vendors, customers, and
financial figures stay consistent across tabs.

M3 — Project Portfolio
- buildProjectPortfolioMock() derives schedule + risk metrics from the
  shared JCC job set.
- UI: Portfolio summary KPI strip, Schedule Slippage Impact card (5
  bucket counts + KPIs + top-5 slipping jobs by exposure), sortable
  Job Profitability table, Top/Bottom Jobs (by margin), and a Risk
  Flags table with severity-tiered chips (Negative Margin, Cost Drift,
  High Commitment Exposure, Late-Stage Margin Risk, Early Cost Drift).

M4 — Commitments & Forecast
- buildCommitmentsForecastMock() emits per-job EAC forecast,
  commitment exposure, change-order activity (most jobs have COs, some
  don't), and a list of open POs/Subs across vendors.
- UI: EAC summary KPI strip + per-job forecast table, Commitment
  Exposure by job (color-coded % committed), Change Order Impact card
  that always renders with a "No data" empty-state when no jobs have
  CO activity (locked design decision), and an Open Commitments table
  with filter pills (All/Past Due/Closing Soon/POs/Subs).

M5 — Billing & Cash
- buildBillingCashMock() reuses JCC + CF outputs so vendors and
  customers stay consistent. Derives billed-to-date, cash collected,
  AP outstanding, and net cash per job; splits AR/AP into aging
  buckets; and produces a priority action list (collect vs pay) sorted
  by weighted aging score.
- UI: Billing & Cash summary KPI strip + per-job table with %-billed
  and net-cash color coding, AR by Job and AP by Job aging tables,
  and a Collections & Payments Priority list with filter pills.

Other:

- All four construction tab placeholders are now replaced with the
