## What is Multi-Factor Authentication?

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is an enhanced security feature that requires **two steps** to access your account:

1. **Your Password** - Something you know
2. **A Verification Code** - A unique 6-digit code from your smartphone

Think of it like your bank's ATM: you need both your card (something you have) and your PIN (something you know) to access your account. Even if someone steals one, they can't access your money without both.

---

## Why MFA Protects Your Financial Data

### The Problem MFA Solves

- **80% of data breaches** involve compromised passwords
- Passwords can be:
  - Stolen through phishing emails
  - Guessed if they're weak
  - Leaked from other websites
  - Intercepted on unsecured networks

### How MFA Stops Unauthorized Access

Even if someone obtains your password, they **cannot** access your account without:
- Your personal smartphone
- The authenticator app installed on it
- The unique 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds

**Result:** Your financial data remains secure even if your password is compromised.

## How It Works - Simple Steps

### One-Time Setup (5 Minutes)

1. **Download a Free Authenticator App**
   - Google Authenticator (recommended)
   - Microsoft Authenticator
   - Authy
   - Any TOTP-compatible app

2. **Scan the QR Code**
   - Open the app on your smartphone
   - Scan the QR code we provide
   - Your app will display a 6-digit code

3. **Verify & Complete**
   - Enter the code to confirm setup
   - Save your backup codes in a safe place
   - You're protected! ✅

### Every Login After Setup

1. Enter your email and password
2. Open your authenticator app
3. Enter the current 6-digit code (changes every 30 seconds)
4. Access your account securely

**Total Time:** 5-10 seconds per login

## Convenient & Secure: Trusted Devices

### "Remember This Device" Feature

For your convenience, you can mark your regular work computer as a **Trusted Device**.

**How It Works:**
- Check "Remember this device for 60 days" when logging in
- MFA is **skipped** on that device for 60 days
- You'll still need MFA on other devices or after 60 days

**Security Features:**
- Maximum 5 trusted devices
- Email notification when a new device is added
- Revoke devices anytime from your settings
- Automatic expiration after 60 days

**Best Practice:**  
✅ Use on your personal work computer  
❌ Don't use on shared or public computers

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is MFA mandatory?**  
A: Yes, for your protection. All accounts require MFA to ensure your financial data remains secure.

**Q: What if I don't have a smartphone?**  
A: You can use tablet-based authenticator apps or contact our support team for alternative options.

**Q: What if I get a new phone?**  
A: Transfer your authenticator app to your new phone, or use a backup code to log in and set up MFA on your new device.

**Q: Will MFA slow me down?**  
A: The verification code takes 5-10 seconds to enter. With the "Trusted Device" feature on your regular computer, you'll only need it every 60 days.

**Q: Is my data secure if I lose my phone?**  
A: Yes! Use one of your backup codes to access your account, then set up MFA on your new device. Without your backup codes, no one can access your account even with your phone.

**Q: Can I turn off MFA?**  
A: No, MFA is mandatory to ensure the security of your sensitive financial information. This protects both you and your business.

**Q: What happens if I can't access my authenticator app?**  
A: Use one of your backup codes, or contact our support team for assistance with account recovery.
